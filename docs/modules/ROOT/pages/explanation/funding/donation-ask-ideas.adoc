= Donation ask ideas
:description: On-site fundraising patterns that show raised amount and the floor or goal — not only a Donate button. Canonical writeup lives on Nonprofit Resources.
:page-aliases: business-bootstrap::funding/explanation/donation-ask-ideas.adoc

Donation-supported orgs (nonprofits, OSS collectives, products that ask instead of charging) usually fail the ask in the same way: a mission sentence and a button, often on someone else's site. Visitors never see **where the fund is** or **what “enough” means**.

The ideas below are page patterns. Payment rails (Stripe, Open Collective, GitHub Sponsors, a fiscal host) can stay wherever accounting already works. The *ask* should stay on your property.

The full operator guide lives on Nonprofit Resources so DevCentr does not grow a second fundraising handbook:

== Idea: operating fund as a ledger

Show, on one page:

* **Balance or raised** (cash in the named fund, with an as-of date)
* **Floor** (minimum to keep the service running for the period) and/or a campaign **goal**
* **Ask** (donate / sponsor) without a hop to a blank third-party form
* **Work the money buys** (a queue, a milestone list, a public budget line)

link:https://www.transparencyhoa.org/analyze-queue[Transparency HOA’s Analyze Queue] is the clearest public example we have on file: monthly cost of the Analyze service (~$6,000), Analysis Fund balance ($7,881 as of 30 April 2026 in the capture below), Donate Now, and the pending work list on the same view.

image::transparencyhoa-analyze-queue.png["Transparency HOA Analyze Queue: fund balance, monthly cost, Donate Now, and pending associations"]

Screenshot of the public page, captured 2026, used as a cited example. Source: link:https://www.transparencyhoa.org/analyze-queue[transparencyhoa.org/analyze-queue].

A surplus above the floor is still useful to show. Honesty beats a crisis headline.

This pattern fits **any** donation model, not only 501(c)(3). Tax receipts change with the legal wrapper; the UX does not.

== Other ask shapes (stubs)

These are cousins, not replacements. Expand when someone implements them.

[cols="1,2",options="header"]
|===
| Shape | When it helps

| Campaign thermometer **with a floor line**
| You have a dated drive (server, legal fund, one grant match). Show the floor you must hit *and* the stretch goal so the bar is not a vanity target.

| Public collective budget
| Multi-maintainer money that must be invoiced and spent in the open. link:https://opencollective.com/[Open Collective] already *is* the ledger — embed or link the budget next to your own ask, do not hide it behind “Sponsor us.”

| Sponsors goal + what it unlocks
| GitHub-native audience. A dollar goal is weak unless you say what crosses the line (N hours/week, a second maintainer, the next CI bill).

| Credits vs cash, labeled
| Cloud/CI grants lower burn but do not pay rent. If you publish a floor, say which part is cash.

| Paid access (not a donation)
| Different problem: entitlements, stores, Stripe. See xref:home::explanation/architecture/subscription-management-platforms.adoc[Subscription management platforms] on the docs hub.
|===
