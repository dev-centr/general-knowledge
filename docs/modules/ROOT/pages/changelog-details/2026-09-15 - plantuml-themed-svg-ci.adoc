== Why

Kroki PlantUML SVG embeds stay fixed-color under the site dark-mode toggle.
A PlantUML adapter sibling of the Mermaid Themed SVG path was needed in CI.

== Change

* Depend on `@dev-centr/plantuml-svg-css-vars` (`github:dev-centr/plantuml-svg-css-vars#v0.1.0` until npm Trusted Publishing is registered for the new package).
* Add `scripts/build-plantuml-diagrams.mjs` and `diagrams:build:plantuml`.
* Rebuild `label-as-wire-break` from `.puml` through Kroki + the adapter.
